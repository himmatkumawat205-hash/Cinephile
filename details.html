Overview

In 2006, journalist Chris Hansen hosts the show To Catch a Predator on NBC, conducting sting operations with the help of an anti-pedophile vigilante group called Perverted-Justice, led by Xavier Von Erck. Luring would-be predators to a house where they expect to engage in sex with a minor (played in person by a decoy actor of legal age), they are confronted by Hansen and his camera crew before being arrested by police. Hansen and his producer, Andie Bender, meet with NBC head Jeff Zucker, who acknowledges the show's ratings success despite his personal distaste for its content and puts it in the primetime slot, competing with ABC's hit show Lost.
